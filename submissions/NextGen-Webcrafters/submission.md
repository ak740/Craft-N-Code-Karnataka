**1. What made you choose this problem?**
I chose this problem because high school students often waste hours studying material they already understand, while completely missing their hidden foundational gaps in Math and Science. Traditional education is one-size-fits-all, leaving students overwhelmed when they fall behind. I wanted to build a solution that shifts the focus from "studying everything" to "studying exactly what you need" by instantly pinpointing conceptual weaknesses and creating a targeted, highly efficient feedback loop to fix them.  



**2. Who is it for, and how would they use it in a normal week?**

Who it is for: It is designed specifically for high school students (Grades 9–12) who are looking to improve their fundamentals in Mathematics and Science.  
How they use it in a normal week:
 Monday (The Baseline): The student logs in and reviews their personal SWOT analysis (Strengths, Weaknesses, Areas of improvement), which is generated from their initial diagnostic test.  
 Tuesday–Thursday (Targeted Training): Instead of reading a whole textbook, they go directly to their identified weak spots (e.g., Algebra). They read bite-sized study materials tailored to that topic and take short practice quizzes to reinforce the concept.  
 Friday (The Check-In): They take their scheduled weekly test to track their progress.  
 Ongoing: Their score updates, and they earn XP and unlock badges (like "Weekly Warrior" or "Topic Master") to maintain their study streak and stay motivated.  




**3. If you had another month, what would you build next?**

If I had another month to expand KinetIQ, I would focus on three major additions:
 Interactive AI Explanations: Currently, when a student gets a question wrong, the app shows them the correct mathematical breakdown. I would build an interactive AI chat step that actively walks them through why they got it wrong, acting as a real-time conversational tutor.  
 Teacher & Parent Dashboards: I would build an external portal that allows educators or guardians to securely view a student's SWOT analysis and weekly progress graphs, allowing them to provide better offline support.  
 Advanced Gamification: While the app already has badges and XP, I would add a "peer challenge" feature where students can compete in live, low-stakes quizzes on mutual weak topics to make learning more collaborative.
